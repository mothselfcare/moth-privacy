account deletion

Delete your Moth account

If you created a Moth account, you can request deletion of that account and the cloud data associated with it.

How to request deletion

Email support@mothselfcare.com with the subject Delete my Moth account. If possible, send the request from the email address associated with your Moth account.

We may ask you to verify that you own the account before completing the request.

request account deletion

What is deleted

Account deletion is intended to remove the Moth account and associated cloud data, including supported cloud-backed progress and user-created content linked to that account.

Information stored only on your device is controlled locally and may need to be removed from the app or device separately.

Retention

Moth retains account and cloud-backup data while your account remains active so account, backup, and synchronization features can work. After an account-deletion request is completed, associated account and cloud data is deleted unless limited retention is required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Questions?

If you have questions about deletion or privacy, contact support@mothselfcare.com or read Moth's Privacy Policy.
