inside Moth

built for ordinary days.

You don't need a perfect routine to use Moth. Open it when you feel like doing a quest or reading something interesting. Write something down if you want to.

something for right now

Your feed mixes affirmations, quests, vocabulary, fun facts, and prompts, so you aren't looking at the same kind of card every time.

a world that grows

As you spend days with Moth, your days in a row can grow, milestones can unlock, and new moths can be discovered. Every moth in Moth is a real species that exists in the world.

keep what matters

Like something? Save it. You can also keep reflections and memories in your Memory Jar so you can find them again later.

our story

why i made Moth

Moth started because I wanted somewhere I could go when I felt like scrolling and actually get something out of the time I spent there. At first it was mostly moths, affirmations, and little quests. I just kept adding things to it from there.
