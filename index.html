Moth Privacy Policy

Effective September 16, 2026

Moth is a self-care app designed to provide affirmations, quests, reflections, discoveries, and other personal wellness experiences. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Moth.

Information stored on your device

Moth stores most app information locally on your device. This may include:

  • your app progress and streak information;
  • liked affirmations;
  • custom affirmations and quests you create;
  • quest responses and reflections;
  • Memory Jar entries and quest history;
  • moths, achievements, and discoveries you have unlocked;
  • your selected categories, appearance settings, and other preferences; and
  • other information necessary to remember your progress within the app.

Moth does not currently require you to create a Moth account, and Moth does not operate a cloud database that stores your personal affirmations, reflections, or other user-created journal content.

Trusted time service

Moth may contact a trusted-time service in order to obtain reliable date and time information. This helps features such as streaks, seasonal content, discoveries, and other date-based experiences work correctly.

Moth does not intentionally send your affirmations, quest responses, reflections, Memory Jar content, or other user-created journal content to the trusted-time service.

Like most internet services, the hosting provider for this service may automatically process standard network information, such as an IP address and basic request information, in order to deliver and protect the service.

Sharing

Moth may allow you to share certain content using your device's built-in sharing features. Sharing only occurs when you choose to use a sharing feature. The destination you select, such as another app or service, may handle that information according to its own privacy practices.

Data sales and advertising

Moth does not sell your personal information.

Moth does not use your personal reflections or other user-created journal content for targeted advertising.

Deleting your information

Moth includes an option to restart your progress, which removes much of your saved journey and progress information while keeping certain app settings and basic app information.

You can also remove locally stored Moth data by uninstalling the app, subject to any device or operating-system backup features you have enabled.

Security

We take reasonable steps to limit the information Moth sends outside your device and to keep personal self-care content local whenever possible. However, no device, storage system, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Moth changes or adds new features. When the policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.
