Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice

Effective September 24, 2026

This notice supplements Moth's Privacy Policy and explains how Moth handles information that may qualify as consumer health data under laws such as the Washington My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer-health-data law.

Moth is a self-care and general-wellness app, not a medical service. Information does not have to be a medical record to qualify as consumer health data under some laws.

Consumer health data Moth may collect

Depending on how you use Moth and what you choose to write, consumer health data may include:

  • reflections, Memory Jar entries, quest responses and quest history that contain information about mood, stress, sleep, body image, health, wellness, relaxation, or similar topics;
  • custom affirmations and custom quests containing comparable information;
  • category choices, reading or interaction information, or personalization signals that reveal health or wellness interests;
  • account-linked progress or usage context when it reveals or is associated with health or wellness information; and
  • health or wellness information you choose to include in a support or privacy request.

Moth does not currently connect to HealthKit, wearable-health feeds, medical records, symptom trackers, or comparable external health-data services.

Sources of consumer health data

Moth receives this information directly from you when you write, choose, save, respond to, or interact with features in Moth. Moth may also derive limited app context from those interactions when needed to provide the feature you requested. Moth does not purchase consumer health data from data brokers.

How Moth uses and processes it

Moth uses consumer health data only for the purposes described here: to provide the self-care, reflection, personalization, progress, and other app features you choose to use; to provide optional cloud backup, synchronization, and restoration when you separately enable that feature; to respond to support or privacy requests you initiate; and to protect, maintain, troubleshoot, and comply with legal obligations for those requested services.

Much of Moth works with data stored on your device. Certain private-writing records stored locally by Moth are encrypted on the device. Some wellness-related personalization signals remain device-local. Signing in alone does not enable ordinary cloud backup.

If you separately enable cloud backup, Moth asks you to expressly consent to Moth storing and syncing supported private self-care content that may include health or wellness information. Moth records that consent before the ordinary cloud-save service can read or write your cloud save.

What Moth shares and with whom

The categories of consumer health data that may be disclosed are limited to the information needed for the requested function:

  • supported signed-in user-created content, quest history and related progress may be processed by cloud infrastructure and authentication providers acting for Moth to provide backup, synchronization and account services;
  • information you choose to send in a support or privacy email may be processed by Moth's email/support provider so the communication can be handled; and
  • content you intentionally send through your device's share feature may be provided to the app or service you select.

Moth does not currently have an affiliate that receives consumer health data. Moth does not disclose consumer health data to advertising or analytics providers for their independent use.

No sale, targeted advertising, or health-location geofencing

Moth does not sell consumer health data, use it for targeted advertising, or use consumer health data to create geofences around health-care services.

Cross-site collection

Moth does not authorize third parties to collect consumer health data about you over time and across unrelated websites or online services when you use Moth's app or website.

Your consumer-health privacy choices

Moth makes the following request options available for consumer health data, subject to authentication and applicable law:

  • confirm whether Moth is collecting, sharing, or selling consumer health data about you and request access to Moth-controlled consumer health data associated with you;
  • request a list of applicable third parties or affiliates with whom your consumer health data was shared or sold;
  • withdraw cloud-backup consent at any time in Moth settings; withdrawal stops future ordinary cloud synchronization and deletes the ordinary server cloud-save copy, while local data remains on your device;
  • withdraw other consent or ask Moth to stop future collection, sharing, or sale where that right applies;
  • request deletion of consumer health data;
  • review and request correction of Moth-controlled consumer health data when correction is technically meaningful; and
  • appeal a refusal to act on a consumer-health request.

Moth does not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable consumer-health privacy right.

How to make a request

Email support@mothselfcare.com with the subject Consumer Health Data Request and tell us which right you want to exercise. If the request concerns a signed-in account, include the ordinary account email or identifier needed to locate it. Please do not send the text of private reflections, diagnoses, symptoms, or other health details just to verify your identity.

If you are signed in and want to delete your Moth account and associated cloud data, you can also use Moth's authenticated Delete Account control or the account deletion page. Moth will not require you to create a new account just to make a privacy request.

Moth may take reasonable steps to verify that the request is yours, preferably using information already associated with your existing account or communication channel.

Review and correction

When Moth already provides an edit control for information you created, you may use that control. For Moth-controlled account-linked information that can be meaningfully corrected, you can request a review by email. If a record cannot reasonably be corrected in place, deletion and recreation may be the appropriate method. Moth will not rewrite the substance of a private reflection as though it were an accuracy error.

Deletion and timing

Moth responds to authenticated consumer-health requests within the time required by applicable law. Where Washington or Nevada consumer-health law applies, the general response period is no later than 45 days, with one additional 45-day extension when legally permitted and reasonably necessary.

For deletion, Moth removes responsive data from active Moth-controlled systems and notifies affected processors or other recipients when required. Consumer health data that exists only on your device cannot be remotely inspected or deleted by Moth; Moth can instead explain the relevant local deletion or reset control. Applicable law may permit archived or backup copies to be deleted later when necessary to restore a backup system. For Washington-covered requests, that backup delay will not exceed six months after authentication.

Appeals

If Moth refuses to act on a consumer-health request, you may appeal by emailing support@mothselfcare.com with the subject Consumer Health Appeal and briefly identifying the earlier request. Moth will respond to an appeal within the period required by applicable law, including within 45 days where Washington or Nevada consumer-health law applies. If an appeal remains denied, Moth will provide the applicable method for contacting the relevant state attorney general when required.

Changes to this notice

Moth will update this notice before beginning a materially new consumer-health-data category, purpose, or recipient that must be disclosed. Moth already requires a separate explicit opt-in before ordinary cloud backup of supported private self-care content begins. If applicable law requires additional affirmative consent for a new use, Moth will ask for that consent before the new processing begins; separate sharing consent will be used where required.

For material changes affecting consumer health data Moth already processes, Moth will update the effective date and provide additional notice in the app, by email, on the website, or through another reasonable method when appropriate or legally required.

Contact

Moth is operated by Moth Self Care LLC. For consumer-health privacy questions or requests, contact support@mothselfcare.com.
