Teen Privacy

Effective September 24, 2026

Moth is intended for people age 13 and older. This page explains Moth's privacy approach for teens in plainer language. It supplements the full Privacy Policy and Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice.

The same high-privacy defaults apply to everyone

Moth does not normally ask for your date of birth just to decide whether you deserve stronger privacy. The app uses a high-privacy baseline for everyone.

  • Moth does not sell personal information or use personal information from the app for targeted advertising.
  • Moth has no public profiles, follower graph, comments, chat, or direct messages.
  • Moth does not have a precise-location feature.
  • Moth does not use a production analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
  • Moth does not infer your age from your writing style, reflections, or behavior in the app.

Most personal use starts on your device

Things such as preferences, progress, likes, custom content, quest responses, and Memory Jar entries can be stored on your device. Certain private-writing records stored locally by Moth use the app's encrypted-storage protection.

Moth does not use private writing to build an advertising profile or to train a recommendation model about you.

Signing in is optional

You can use Moth without creating a Moth account. If you choose to sign in, signing in opens your account but does not turn on ordinary cloud backup of your private self-care content. Cloud backup stays off until you separately enable it.

Private writing can contain information about health or wellness. Before ordinary cloud backup can store or sync supported private self-care content, Moth asks for a separate explicit consent statement that names that sensitive possibility. You can later turn cloud backup off in settings; Moth then stops future ordinary cloud synchronization and deletes the ordinary server cloud-save copy while keeping your local data and account. The Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice explains this in more detail.

Your privacy controls

You can remove or restart portions of local progress using Moth's controls. If you have an account, you can request deletion through Moth's authenticated Delete Account control, the account deletion page, or by contacting support.

For a privacy question or request, email support@mothselfcare.com. You do not need to send the text of a private reflection, diagnosis, symptom, or other sensitive detail just to prove who you are.

Visual progression is optional

Moth has quiet visual progression such as Days in a Row, plant growth, moth discoveries, achievements, and collectibles. These features are cosmetic and do not make your account stronger, create a public ranking, affect other users, or have cash value. You do not need to maintain a streak or collect anything to use Moth's core self-care, reading, reflection, or learning features.

Some discoveries or visual milestones can unfold across more than one visit or day. Moth does not sell streak protection or faster progression, and it does not use public leaderboards or social streak rankings.

If you are under 13

Please do not create a Moth account or provide personal information through Moth. Moth does not operate a parental-consent program for children under 13. If a parent, guardian, or other person believes a child under 13 provided personal information through Moth, contact support@mothselfcare.com so it can be reviewed and removed as appropriate.

Parents, guardians, and local-law requirements

Moth does not provide a hidden parental-monitoring feature. If the law where you live requires a parent or guardian to consent before a teen uses Moth or a particular feature, that requirement still applies.

If Moth ever adds age assurance

If Moth later needs an age-assurance step, Moth will explain what is being collected and why. Age-assurance information must be limited to the age-related purpose, kept only as long as reasonably necessary, and not reused as an advertising or personalization signal.

Questions

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact support@mothselfcare.com.
